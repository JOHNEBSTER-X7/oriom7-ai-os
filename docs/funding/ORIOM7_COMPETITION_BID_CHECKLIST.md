# ORIOM7 — Competition-Specific Bid Checklist

This checklist is deliberately competition-neutral. Complete it afresh for each live funding opportunity.

## A. Call fit
- [ ] Confirm the call is open on the intended submission date and note the exact deadline/time zone.
- [ ] Confirm the legal applicant type, UK registration requirement and eligible company size.
- [ ] Confirm whether a single SME applicant is allowed or collaboration is mandatory.
- [ ] Confirm the call's research theme, TRL, novelty and exploitation requirements.
- [ ] Confirm project-cost minimum/maximum, duration, start/end dates and geography.
- [ ] Check exclusions: routine integration, generic agent orchestration, subcontracting, cross-cutting scope or other disallowed work.
- [ ] Save a dated copy/link of the official competition guidance and terms.

## B. Company and financial eligibility
- [ ] Companies House record and company number.
- [ ] SME test including partner/linked enterprises.
- [ ] Shareholders, PSCs, ownership/control and group structure.
- [ ] Latest filed accounts and current management accounts.
- [ ] Cash-flow forecast covering grant payment timing and working capital.
- [ ] Public funding/subsidy register and declarations.
- [ ] Match-funding source, amount, availability date and documentary proof.
- [ ] Board approval for the application and any match commitment.

## C. Technical eligibility and novelty
- [ ] State the technical uncertainty in one precise research question.
- [ ] Explain why existing methods do not already solve it.
- [ ] Provide prior-art and competitor literature review.
- [ ] Separate existing product capability from proposed research.
- [ ] Define a testable hypothesis and measurable outcomes.
- [ ] Pre-register baselines, ablations, negative controls and test splits.
- [ ] Provide evidence that the proposed work is not routine integration or prompt engineering.
- [ ] Explain IP ownership, freedom-to-operate checks and exploitation route.

## D. Costing and delivery
- [ ] Staff costs linked to named people, rates and time allocation.
- [ ] Compute/cloud/equipment costs supported by usage assumptions and call rules.
- [ ] Overheads calculated using the permitted method.
- [ ] Travel/materials/other costs justified and within call rules.
- [ ] Subcontracting only where expressly permitted and procured appropriately.
- [ ] Grant and match calculated from the exact call intervention rate.
- [ ] Cash timing and reimbursement risk modelled.
- [ ] Milestones, dependencies, risks and contingency documented.

## E. Data, ethics, security and accessibility
- [ ] Dataset provenance and source licences.
- [ ] UK GDPR/Data Protection Act assessment where personal data is processed.
- [ ] DPIA screening and lawful-basis review where applicable.
- [ ] Third-party model and search provider terms, retention and residency reviewed.
- [ ] Security threat model, access controls and incident process.
- [ ] Human review and escalation for uncertain or conflicting evidence.
- [ ] Accessibility preservation and user testing plan.
- [ ] No unsupported claims of factual guarantees, certification or audit completion.

## F. Submission and audit trail
- [ ] Application answers map directly to scoring criteria.
- [ ] Budget totals reconcile across all forms and work packages.
- [ ] All claims trace to code, test output, document or named owner.
- [ ] Commit SHAs and release tags recorded for technical evidence.
- [ ] Internal red-team review completed.
- [ ] Submission receipt and final submitted copy archived.

## Go/no-go

Do not submit if the company is ineligible, the research does not match the call, the match funding is unproven, costs are ineligible, or the application relies on claims that cannot be evidenced. A technically strong repository cannot compensate for an ineligible applicant or a non-compliant budget.
