## 3. What this means

The company-number test is passed: SPECTRUM X LIMITED is a registered UK private limited company and the public register displays it as Active.

That does **not** establish full Innovate UK eligibility. In particular, SME classification, financial viability, company control, IP title and match funding remain unverified.

The overdue accounts and confirmation statement are the most urgent company-level risks. They are not described here as automatic disqualifiers under the competition rules, but they create avoidable due-diligence concerns. Innovate UK states that it conducts financial viability and eligibility checks and cannot award to businesses in financial difficulty. The previous strike-off notice makes it especially important to explain the history accurately and demonstrate that the company is compliant now.

The SIC code is a descriptive register classification, not proof that the company cannot undertake AI R&D. Do not change it merely to look eligible. If the company's genuine business activities have changed or diversified, make an accurate update through the proper Companies House process.

The competition permits a single UK-registered SME applicant, requires £50,000–£150,000 eligible costs and 1–3 months duration, and prohibits subcontractors. It requires genuinely novel reusable AI mechanisms and experimental validation.

This call is therefore still open on the date of this assessment, but the deadline is imminent. Do not submit unless the company filing position, SME status, finances, research novelty, costs and match funding can all be evidenced in time. The published call estimates a 4% chance of success.
