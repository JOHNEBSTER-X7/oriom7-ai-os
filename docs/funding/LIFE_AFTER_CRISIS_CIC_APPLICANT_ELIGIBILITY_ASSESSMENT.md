## 3. CIC and Innovate UK classification — critical distinction

A CIC is a limited company, but CIC status alone does not prove that it qualifies as an SME business for a particular Innovate UK competition.

Innovate UK defines a business as an enterprise undertaking economic activities. Its funding rules distinguish businesses, research organisations, public-sector organisations/charities and third-sector participants. The relevant classification depends on the organisation's actual activity and the competition rules. The SME calculation must include relevant ownership, interests, associations and linked enterprises.

The UK Government's SME guidance states that the key test is engagement in economic activity rather than legal form. Therefore, Life After Crisis may be capable of qualifying as an SME if it undertakes economic activity and meets the applicable size and relationship tests; this must be evidenced, not assumed.

Do not assume the CIC qualifies for the 100% funding treatment available to certain public-sector organisations or charities undertaking non-economic research. A CIC is not automatically a registered charity, and the proposed competition requires a UK-registered SME as a sole applicant.

## 5. Competition fit and deadline

The official Innovate UK competition **Efficient, Structured and Controllable AI Systems** closes **18 November 2026 at 11:00 UK time**. It allows a single applicant only where the lead is a UK-registered micro, small or medium-sized enterprise. Eligible project costs are £50,000–£150,000, duration is 1–3 months, subcontractors are not allowed, and the project must experimentally validate novel reusable AI mechanisms. The call excludes routine integration, generic agent orchestration and prompt engineering without novel AI/ML development.

ORIOM7's proposed learned uncertainty-aware controller may be framed under resource/data adaptive learning or learned modular computation/routing, but only if the technical novelty, falsifiable hypothesis, baselines, metrics, experimental plan, defensible foreground IP and delivery capacity are made explicit. The current ORIOM7 architecture is a baseline; the proposed controller must not be described as already implemented or validated.

Do not submit unless the CIC's SME/business classification, confirmation statement filing position, community-purpose fit, financial viability, IP chain-of-title, match funding and research plan can all be evidenced before the deadline.
